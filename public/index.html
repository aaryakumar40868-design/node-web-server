Pro Tips & Features

  • Pinning: Mark critical notes as pinned to keep them pinned at the top of your vault.
  • Favorites: Click the star icon on any note or task to add it to your Starred view.
  • Calendar Sync: Tasks with due dates automatically render on the calendar days grid.
  • Data Portability: Use the Export feature in Settings to download your entire database as a JSON backup anytime.

Data Persistence & Offline

Your notes and tasks are preserved locally via localStorage, meaning you will never lose work upon browser refresh or server restarts.
